A PRACTICAL STARTING POINT

A welcome sequence should answer the subscriber's request before it tries to sell.

Start with a clear opt-in, deliver what was promised, and make each follow-up useful and easy to stop. This simple structure works across email platforms; the exact settings and legal requirements depend on your tools and audience.

01
Confirm what the person asked to receive and record the source of the opt-in.
02
Deliver the promised resource with a recognizable sender and a clear next step.
03
Measure delivery, engagement, and unsubscribes, then improve one thing at a time.
