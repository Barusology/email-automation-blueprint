About The Email Automation Blueprint

The Email Automation Blueprint is an educational resource for people who want to plan, build, and test permission-based email workflows. The site starts with a free, platform-independent guide and also offers a separate paid digital workbook with additional projects and editable materials.

What the resources cover

The free guides focus on practical workflow planning, permission and subscriber expectations, testing, troubleshooting, and measurement. Examples are illustrative; software interfaces and legal requirements can change and vary by platform and location. The material is general education, not legal advice or a promise of business results.

We aim to keep instructions clear, distinguish examples from guarantees, and link to primary guidance where a topic depends on changing platform rules. If you find an outdated instruction or a broken link, please let us know so it can be reviewed.
