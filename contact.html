SEC. 04

Before you write

A little context up front means a faster, more useful answer. Include whichever of these apply:

  • Order or refund request: the email used at checkout and your Creem receipt or order ID.
  • Broken link or outdated step: the page URL, the section name, and what you expected to find.
  • Automation question: your email platform, the workflow trigger, and what actually happened versus what you expected.
  • Never send: passwords, payment card numbers, or other sensitive credentials — support will never ask for them.
